Your Fitbit data, yours to explore.

fitdata downloads your data from Fitbit through the Google Health API every hour and shows it on a clean dashboard: sleep, heart rate, HRV, blood oxygen and activity, day by day and over time. Connect Claude or ChatGPT and ask questions about it in plain language.

One day at a glance

Last night's sleep stages, heart rate through the day, HRV, SpO₂, steps and workouts on one page.

Trends over months

Resting heart rate, HRV, sleep and activity over a week, a month, a quarter or your whole history.

Ask your AI assistant

A read-only MCP server lets Claude or ChatGPT look at your data when you ask them to, and only yours.

How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in with Google

    The Google account your Fitbit uses. fitdata only learns your e-mail address and account ID.

  2. 2

    Connect Fitbit

    Grant read-only access to activity, health metrics, sleep and your profile. fitdata never writes to your account.

  3. 3

    Explore

    Your whole history is downloaded once, then the latest data every hour.

Your data stays yours

  • Read-only access. Only the four Google Health scopes the dashboard needs.
  • Never sold, never for ads. Your data isn't shared with anyone or used to train AI models.
  • Stored in the EU. On Google Cloud in Belgium, encrypted; your access token additionally with a dedicated key.
  • Delete it any time. One click removes all your data and revokes fitdata's access.
