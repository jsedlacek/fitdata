How it works

  1. 1

    Sign in with Google

    Allow Fitdata to read your Fitbit data. It can't change anything, and your history is ready in a few minutes.

  2. 2

    Read your dashboard

    Last night and today on one page, trends over months, and a (?) next to every number that says what it means.

  3. 3

    Ask your AI

    Add Fitdata as a connector in Claude or ChatGPT (two-minute setup), then ask:

    • I feel off. Am I getting sick?
    • Do evening runs change my deep sleep?
    • Is my resting heart rate going down?

Less pep talk. More data.
