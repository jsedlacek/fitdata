Privacy policy

Last updated 25 October 2026

Fitdata (fitdata.jakub.contact) is a personal dashboard for your Fitbit data. It is run by Jakub Sedláček, an individual in the Czech Republic, who is the controller of your personal data. Questions and requests go to fitdata@jakub.contact.

What we collect

  • Your Google Health user ID, a number the Google Health API identifies you by. We use it to know who you are and to keep your data apart from everyone else's. Fitdata doesn't ask Google for your name, e-mail address or other account details.
  • Your health and fitness data, read from the Google Health API after you choose when you sign in and grant access on Google's consent screen (or later with Connect Fitbit). Fitdata asks only for these read-only scopes:
    • googlehealth.activity_and_fitness.readonly: steps, distance, floors, energy burned, active and active zone minutes, sedentary periods, exercise sessions;
    • googlehealth.health_metrics_and_measurements.readonly: heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO₂), respiratory rate, skin temperature during sleep, VO₂ max, weight, height, body fat and blood glucose;
    • googlehealth.sleep.readonly: sleep sessions and stages;
    • googlehealth.profile.readonly: the date your account was created, so we know how far back your history goes.
  • Access tokens: the token Google issues so Fitdata can download new data every hour. It is stored encrypted with a dedicated Cloud KMS key; the web server can encrypt it but not read it.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider records requests (time, address, path, IP address and browser) to keep the service secure and working. These logs are kept for 30 days.

We don't collect anything else: no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising identifiers.

How we use it

Only to provide Fitdata to you: downloading your data, turning it into the tables behind your dashboard, showing it to you, and answering the AI assistants you connect yourself. We don't sell your data, show you ads, build profiles of you, use your data to train AI or machine learning models, or let anyone read it. The only exceptions are fixing a problem you ask us to look into, with your consent, keeping the service secure, and complying with the law.

Fitdata's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Legal basis

Health data is a special category of personal data under the GDPR. We process it on the basis of your explicit consent (Articles 6(1)(a) and 9(2)(a) GDPR), which you give on Google's consent screen and can withdraw at any time by deleting your account or revoking Fitdata's access in your Google account. Your user ID is processed to provide the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b)), and logs for our legitimate interest in a secure service (Article 6(1)(f)).

Who else sees it

  • Google Cloud hosts Fitdata and stores your data as our processor, in the europe-west1 region (Belgium, EU).
  • AI assistants you connect. If you add Fitdata to Claude, ChatGPT or another MCP client, that assistant can read your data when you ask it something; what it reads is sent to its provider at your request and is covered by that provider's privacy policy. You can disconnect it at any time in the assistant's settings.

Nobody else. Your data is never shared with other Fitdata users.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS) and data is encrypted at rest. Every request reads only the signed-in user's data, the assistants' queries run in a session that can see only your tables, and only the hourly sync job can decrypt access tokens. The code is open source on GitHub, so you can check how it works.

How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you choose Delete account in Settings, Fitdata revokes its access at Google and deletes your data and your account record, usually within minutes. Deleted files can remain in Google Cloud's deleted-object retention for up to 7 days before they are gone for good. Logs expire after 30 days.

Cookies

Fitdata sets only cookies that it needs to work: fd_session keeps you signed in for up to 30 days and fd_oauth protects the sign-in for 10 minutes. Your choice of light or dark theme is kept in your browser's local storage. No consent banner is needed because nothing is used for tracking.

Your rights

You can download a copy of all your data at any time with Download your data in Settings (a ZIP file with your data as Google returned it, in JSON, and the processed tables), and delete your account there too. You can also ask for a copy of your data, its correction, deletion or a transfer to another service, object to or restrict its processing, and withdraw your consent, by writing to the address above; we will reply within a month. You can also complain to a supervisory authority, in the Czech Republic the Office for Personal Data Protection.

Children

Fitdata is not meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above, and for significant changes let you know on the dashboard before they take effect.
